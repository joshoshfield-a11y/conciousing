% 1. IIT
Integrated Information Theory (IIT 3.0/4.0)
 & \pmark\ (concept repertoire = cause--effect structure)
 & \xmark\ (single temporal grain; $\Phi^{\max}$ over partitions)
 & \stars{5}\ ($\Phi$ = integration measure par excellence)
 & \pmark\ (concepts self-specify; no recursive self-model)
 & \xmark\ (monadic; no higher-order access)
 & \xmark\ (no agency; intrinsic existence)
 & \pmark\ (substrate-independent, needs causal power) \\

% 2. GWT
Global Workspace Theory (Baars, Dehaene)
 & \pmark\ (broadcast enables cross-module simulation)
 & \pmark\ (workspace $\sim$ working-memory seconds)
 & \stars{3}\ (global availability $\approx$ integration; no scalar)
 & \pmark\ (``self'' as workspace content, not structural)
 & \stars{3}\ (conscious access = reportability)
 & \pmark\ (agency via competition for workspace)
 & \pmark\ (neural coalitions; modality-agnostic) \\

% 3. HOT
Higher-Order Thought (Rosenthal, Brown)
 & \xmark\ (HOTs target first-order states)
 & \xmark\ (single level: FO + HO)
 & \pmark\ (HO thought glues FO states; unquantified)
 & \stars{4}\ (explicit HO representation of FO states)
 & \stars{5}\ (metacognition \emph{is} the theory)
 & \xmark\ (agency orthogonal)
 & \xmark\ (representational; no sensorimotor requirement) \\

% 4. AST
Attention Schema Theory (Graziano)
 & \pmark\ (schema models what could be attended)
 & \pmark\ (updated at attentional timescale $\sim$100ms)
 & \pmark\ (schema integrates attention signals)
 & \stars{5}\ (attention schema \emph{is} the self-model)
 & \stars{4}\ (schema = metacognitive access to attention)
 & \pmark\ (attention control = limited agency)
 & \pmark\ (neural attention; sensory/motor) \\

% 5. PP / Active Inference
Predictive Processing / Active Inference (Friston, Clark, Hohwy)
 & \stars{4}\ (hierarchical generative model $\rightarrow$ imagination)
 & \stars{4}\ (explicit multi-timescale hierarchy $\tau_1 \ll \tau_2 \ll \dots$)
 & \stars{3}\ (variational free energy $F$ = integration proxy)
 & \pmark\ (self-model = high-level body priors; implicit)
 & \pmark\ (precision weighting $\approx$ confidence)
 & \stars{5}\ (active inference: action minimizes surprise)
 & \stars{5}\ (embodied sensorimotor grounding essential) \\

% 6. Enactivism
Enactivism / Enaction (Varela, Thompson, Di Paolo, No\"e)
 & \stars{3}\ (sensorimotor contingencies = ``what if I move?'')
 & \pmark\ (timescales = sensorimotor loops)
 & \pmark\ (structural coupling; unquantified)
 & \pmark\ (autopoietic self-distinction; non-representational)
 & \xmark\ (rejects representational metacognition)
 & \stars{5}\ (agency = sense-making, constitutive)
 & \stars{5}\ (radical embodiment; lived body is the ground) \\
